# 06 - Define the Deep Research evidence contract in-product

**What to build:** Deep Research has source-scope preview, evidence-state labels, missing-evidence vocabulary, and tests for unsupported versus cited claims.

**Blocked by:** 01 - Establish built-in workbench mode contract.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Deep Research can preview or summarize the source scope before a run begins.
- [ ] Yue has user-visible labels for source-supported, inferred, user-confirmed, unsupported, and missing-evidence claims.
- [ ] The evidence contract can represent unavailable sources and citation warnings.
- [ ] Tests cover cited claims, unsupported claims, and missing-evidence states.
- [ ] The evidence contract does not silently convert research findings into durable memory.
